02 / Privacy & support

Useful help should not require blind trust.

This is the public privacy promise for Trixie Misheen projects and support conversations.

LOCAL
FIRST

Your work stays yours.

DevSuite is designed to inspect your local development environment. Source code and credentials are not included in support reports by default.

If you choose to export or share a report, review it first: it may contain local executable paths, selected project paths, diagnostics, system information, and recent activity.

Read the full privacy policy ↗

Before you share

  • Read the report preview from top to bottom.
  • Redact names, paths, or activity you do not want to disclose.
  • Share only with someone you trust and only for the support needed.
