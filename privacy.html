Privacy / A plain-language promise

Your work stays yours.

This policy explains what may be visible when you use Trixie Misheen tools, visit this site, or choose to ask for support.

01 / Local by default

Your projects remain on your machine.

DevSuite is a local desktop application. It works with the folders, environments, commands, and tools you choose on your computer. It does not upload your source code or credentials as part of normal local operation.

02 / Support reports

Sharing is your choice.

If you export a support report, it can include executable paths, selected project paths, diagnostics, system information, and recent activity. It is not sent automatically.

Always preview and redact a report before sharing it.

03 / Website contact

Email starts in your own client.

Links on this site use mailto: links. Your email provider handles the message, attachments, retention, and delivery.

04 / Questions

Ask before you share.

If something is unclear, ask what a diagnostic contains or how to remove sensitive information.
